Interface Overview

Brain scans guide life-changing decisions, yet today, up to 40% of clinicians still rely on subjective visual interpretation of the image.

Subtle changes—the earliest signs of disease—are often missed by the human eye.

Veralia Benefits

Identifies Brain Affected Areas

By comparing individual patients against healthy databases, clinicians can immediately identify metabolic abnormalities.

Tracks Disease Progression

It compares scans taken at different time points to monitor patient evolution.

Physician-Centric Interface

Designed specifically for clinical workflows, our intuitive interface ensures rapid adoption without technical complexity.

Reduces Time to Diagnosis

Total diagnostic time is significantly reduced, ensuring patients receive critical treatment interventions faster.

Eliminates Observer Variability

Standardized quantification ensures that metrics remain consistent regardless of the reading clinician.

Automates Processing Pipeline

Manual interventions are removed by integrating directly with hardware, providing immediate results.

Bridging the Gap in Neuroimaging
